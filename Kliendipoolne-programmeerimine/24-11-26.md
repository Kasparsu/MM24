### 1. 🎯 Why a mock exam now

There is roughly a month left, and rather than installing yet more packages, it makes sense
to practise the real thing. Some students had already heard about it; for everyone else it
was posted as the Teams assignment **"Proovieksam"** (due **20.12**), with a short
instruction: do it, hand it in exactly as required, use your own name and pick a variant.

### 2. 🧩 How it works

The exam lives on a small site: <https://tptmmexam2026.surge.sh/#/>. Enter your own first
and last name and it generates **three variants** for you, each with its own theme, client
brand and mascot (the same name always gives the same variants). Choose one of the three and
do the exam for that client.

The exam follows the real multimedia exam's structure, four modules, each handed in as its
own zip:

- **Module 1, design:** a Figma design with a hand made logo and five subpages, in desktop,
  tablet and mobile sizes.
- **Module 2, AI images:** generated images plus a file with the prompts.
- **Module 3, front-end:** the design built as **identical** web pages in HTML, CSS and
  JavaScript (frameworks such as Tailwind, Bootstrap or Vue are allowed), responsive,
  semantic, with responsive and lazy loaded images and a Lighthouse score of at least 90;
  hosting on surge.sh or GitHub Pages gives extra points.
- **Module 4, back-end:** a CMS or a custom backend with an admin login, content forms and
  a working contact form.

All four go into one final zip named after you. `node_modules` (and `vendor`) must not end up
in Git or in the zip.

### 3. ⚖️ Graded like the real exam

The mock exam is graded the same way as the real one, perhaps a little less strictly. The
idea is simple: if you cannot manage this one, there is not much hope for the real exam. On
the other hand there is far more time for it than for the real exam, which also helps with
everything else going on. To get a feel for real exam conditions, you can try setting aside a
week of your own for it, for example over a school holiday or when you are not on
internship.

## ✅ Takeaways

1. **Mock exam ("Proovieksam")**: last year's multimedia exam, due 20.12 in Teams.
2. **Your own name** on the exam site gives three variants: pick one.
