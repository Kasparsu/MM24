### 1. 📋 The understanding check: "Mõistete kontroll ja tagasiside tundidele"

A Forms quiz was posted in Teams as an assignment (due 18.11). It asks about the concepts from
the whole subject: what a model and a controller are, what a view does, how a request travels
through the app, and so on, from the learnphp framework lessons to the Laravel blog. The last
questions are feedback on the lessons and the teacher.

How it is assessed, as explained in the lesson:

- **Passed / not passed only.** Doing it gives a pass, not doing it gives a fail. There is no
  number grade, and wrong answers do not lower anything.
- **Answer honestly and on your own**, without asking an AI or reading notes. The point is to
  see what has really been understood, both for the teacher (what needs explaining again) and for
  the students (where to catch up).
- **Still try to answer correctly.** "I don't know" is a fine answer; random guesses are not.

Everyone was asked to do it right away in the lesson. Once it is submitted, the lesson is over,
and those at home could carry on being at home.
