## 🗂️ Topics Covered

### 1. ✨ Magic Methods: Reference

Pointer into the manual for the magic methods covered in the longer session the same day
(`__construct`, `__get`, `__set`, `__call`, `__invoke`, `__toString`, `__destruct`, and the
rest). Worth skimming the whole list once so you know what exists.

| Link | What it is |
|---|---|
| <https://www.php.net/manual/en/language.oop5.magic.php> | All PHP magic methods |

---

### 2. 🧮 Practice: Project Euler

A large archive of mathematical/programming problems, ordered roughly by difficulty. Each one
is a small, self-contained puzzle with a single numeric answer, so it's a good fit for
practising the language basics (loops, conditionals, arithmetic, functions) without needing
a project around it.

| Link | What it is |
|---|---|
| <https://projecteuler.net/archives> | Project Euler problem archive |

---

### 3. 🎮 Practice: CodinGame

The other suggestion, and a gentler entry point: puzzles presented as games, starting with an
onboarding exercise that walks you through how the platform works. You write code in the
browser and watch it drive the game.

| Link | What it is |
|---|---|
| <https://www.codingame.com/ide/puzzle/onboarding> | CodinGame onboarding puzzle |

![Practice](https://api.memegen.link/images/drake/Reading_about_loops/Solving_puzzles_with_loops.png)

## ✅ Takeaways

1. **Skim the magic methods list** so you recognise them when you meet them.
2. **Project Euler** for maths-flavoured drilling of the fundamentals.
3. **CodinGame** if you'd rather the practice felt like a game. Start with the onboarding
   puzzle.
